What the role asks for, and where I have done it

Set the strategic direction and design vision for a complex area

At Humain I set the product vision and experience principles for Humain One, Saudi Arabia's national AI platform, and ran design reviews against them, so the teams building on it worked to one standard rather than to taste. On Wakeel I set the rule that each recommendation opens to the depth its weight deserves, from one block for a day's leave to costs by item and by year for a three-year tender.

UX principles

Partner with Product and Engineering leaders to turn strategy into execution

On Wakeel each review becomes tasks, options built on the real app code, and a document per task for engineering: what users see, what to build, the data, states and exceptions, and done criteria. All nine tasks of the first review were settled and handed to development. The approval to Oracle went through 17 options in 8 days, and ERP approvals moved onto the desk itself.

Wakeel 03

Build and nurture a high-performing design team

I have led teams of 6 to 25 and built three of them from scratch. At Saudi Aramco I led a 12-person UX team, at Humain I built and led the six-person experience team for Humain One, and at Devsisters I built a 25-person team across product, engineering, art and marketing.

Teams

An exceptional quality bar, through clear feedback and coaching

At Humain, design reviews with the six-person experience team ran against the experience principles I set, so work was judged by one standard rather than by taste. On Wakeel the components carry written states: counts hidden at 0, 99+ above 99, the last value kept while loading, and mirrored for Arabic.

Wakeel, components

Operate at every altitude, from long-term direction to the last detail

I still make things. Wakeel's face is one orb in eight states that read without a visible text label at 58 points, moved by one shader, and it stills under Reduce Motion. I build my own products in code with Claude Code, and I also set their prices, targets and stop rules.

Wakeel, Presence

Payments people trust: clear, honest screens where money moves

On Paldo Saju, showing the real first-time and repeat prices in place of a struck-through discount raised revenue per checkout visitor by 61%, and a price that was never charged is never shown as a comparison price. On TarotMasters, showing KakaoPay, NaverPay and card payment side by side raised payment completion from 54% to 80%. In Wakeel an approval cannot be taken back once it reaches Oracle, so every action waits five seconds first, with undo in the alert above.

In my own practice I rebuilt the way of working around AI: on Wakeel, agents build every option on the real app code, 94 over three reviews, and my review page sends each note, with the sentence it is about, to the agent that made the page.

Designing with AI

Where I would be stretching

I have not led design inside a bank. My banking work was leading UX for Barclays and BNP Paribas from London agencies, and the payment flows I run today are my own, tested on about 150 visitors per side rather than millions of customers. What carries over is the habit: set the quality bar in the open, decide from the funnel, and make the screen where money moves the most honest one in the product.

03Products

What I build and run at Raymind.AI

Beyond productivity, I believe the most important use of AI is its most basic one: conversation that hears people's worries. Paldo Saju, TarotMasters and PastLife are different ways in, each starting from a narrow topic (saju, tarot, past lives) so it can hear worries in detail and answer precisely. What people worry about most, and which answers help them open up, became data for the next step, Auntie Rabbit.

memory.wiki

Gathers your AI conversations and documents at one address and hands them to any AI as context. Web, Mac, iPhone and Android apps, browser and VS Code extensions, CLI and MCP server, built on my own.

Paldo Saju
