Multi-turn conversations that hand over to a person at the right moment

Paldo Saju and TarotMasters are chat consultations where a master hears the worry first and then talks it through. A review of live character chats on IsekaiPlay found replies picked up the user's words 15 times out of 15 but ended on a pull, a question or a request, only 5 times, so I set a rule that the first three replies end on one; it is going into the engine now. In Wakeel the person decides: a quick approve appears only when the checks raise no issue, and anything sent to Oracle waits five seconds with undo. Anees acts only once the user approves, and records every action.

Editorial judgement: what to say, and what to leave out

In Wakeel's first review, approving an Oracle purchase request ended on "Everyone has been notified", and nothing said the work had moved on in Oracle. After 17 options the finished state reads "Approved in Oracle" and shows only what was done, where and when, and what happens next. On IsekaiPlay, a rule now ends a character's first three replies on them reaching out, and keeps side characters out of it; in testing that went from 6 of 15 replies to 15 of 15.

Wakeel 05

Evaluation datasets, scoring and testing plans

Grading 85 readings on TarotMasters showed that 65% contained made-up facts. I wrote a rule that only what the user said counts as fact and tested it on a sample, where it cut that rate from 70% to 30%, and only then rolled it out to everyone. On memory.wiki I designed a benchmark that gives Claude, GPT-4o and Gemini the same material and questions: 95% correct for each, and 93.3% agreement between them.

memory.wiki 03

Conversational safeguards: scope, sensitive information and the authority to act

In Wakeel a quick approve appears only when the checks raise no issue, and anything sent to Oracle waits five seconds with undo in the alert above. Anees acts only once the user approves and records every action. In memory.wiki drafts never go out on a public URL, and permissions are checked where data is served, for browsers and AIs alike.

Show the direction set, the alternatives explored and the outcome

Wakeel's approval to Oracle went through 17 options in 8 days before we chose one, and each review is on record: options built on the real app code, comments that move to done, and an update log of 253 entries recording what changed and why. On Paldo Saju a bundle that won its A/B test was reverted when click-through halved after rollout.

Connect each conversation to the wider service

On Paldo Saju the last free answer ends with two blurred lines that open when you leave an email, and the payment rate rose from 5.8% to 9.2% in an A/B test. Every Wakeel task goes to engineering with what users see, the data, states and exceptions, and done criteria. At Saudi Aramco I designed the whole digital journey for Saudi women getting their first driving licence.

Where I would be stretching

I have written test sets, graded model output and set rules from it, but I have not annotated data for model training or designed the handover from an AI agent to human support staff, and my conversations have been consultations, companions and assistants rather than delivery support across three marketplaces. What carries over is the judgement: decide what the model may say and do, check it against graded output before it reaches everyone, and hand over to a person where the system's authority ends.

03Products

What I build and run at Raymind.AI

Beyond productivity, I believe the most important use of AI is its most basic one: conversation that hears people's worries. Paldo Saju, TarotMasters and PastLife are different ways in, each starting from a narrow topic (saju, tarot, past lives) so it can hear worries in detail and answer precisely. What people worry about most, and which answers help them open up, became data for the next step, Auntie Rabbit.

memory.wiki

Gathers your AI conversations and documents at one address and hands them to any AI as context. Web, Mac, iPhone and Android apps, browser and VS Code extensions, CLI and MCP server, built on my own.
