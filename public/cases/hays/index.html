Earlier work

Generative AI with appropriate controls

A quick approve appears only when the checks raise no issue, every action is logged, and anything sent to Oracle waits five seconds with undo, because an approval cannot be taken back once it reaches Oracle. That flow went through 17 options in 8 days. In my studio only one agent is allowed to deploy, and ad spend and losses have daily caps.

Wakeel 05

LLMs, prompt engineering and evaluation

memory.wiki gives Claude, GPT-4o and Gemini the same material and 20 questions: 95% correct for each, and 93.3% agreement between them. On IsekaiPlay a rule that the first three replies end on a pull went from 6 of 15 to 15 of 15 in testing. On TarotMasters grading 85 readings showed 65% made up facts; fixing the cause, a later instruction overriding the source rule, cut it from 63% to 16% and 5% on the same 19 situations, and the live rate from 39.1% to 21.5%, short of the 10% target.

memory.wiki 04

RAG, vector databases, semantic search and knowledge graphs

memory.wiki splits what it gathers by heading, embeds it into pgvector and returns it with a concept graph the model extracts, so Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor and Codex all read the same context through an MCP server with 29 tools.

memory.wiki 03

AI orchestration, MLOps and cloud AI platforms

The IsekaiPlay story engine gives each job, from classifying a message to writing a key scene, to a model tier in one config file, and can switch it between Google, OpenAI, Anthropic and DeepSeek. Prompt caching cut the cost of each LLM call by 19.5%. About 50 agents with set roles run my studio day to day.

03Products

What I build and run at Raymind.AI

Beyond productivity, I believe the most important use of AI is its most basic one: conversation that hears people's worries. Paldo Saju, TarotMasters and PastLife are different ways in, each starting from a narrow topic (saju, tarot, past lives) so it can hear worries in detail and answer precisely. What people worry about most, and which answers help them open up, became data for the next step, Auntie Rabbit.

memory.wiki

Gathers your AI conversations and documents at one address and hands them to any AI as context. Web, Mac, iPhone and Android apps, browser and VS Code extensions, CLI and MCP server, built on my own.

memory.wiki

Paldo Saju

An AI consultation that starts from saju: master characters from Korea's eight provinces hear your worry first, then talk it through with you. Fixing the checkout by the numbers raised revenue per checkout visitor 61%.

Auntie RabbitIn beta
