01Meta, Product Manager, Agent Enablement

What the role asks for, and where I have done it

Decide what to build next from evals, telemetry and user failure modes

On tarotmasters.app, grading 85 readings showed that 65% contained made-up facts. On a test sample, a rule that only what the user said counts as fact cut that rate from 70% to 30%, and only then did it go out to everyone. On IsekaiPlay, 49% of sessions with a first reply that speaks to the character reached ten turns, against 0% for the old line that just stood there, so first-reply choices now speak to the character. On Paldo Saju, a bundle that won its A/B test was reverted when click-through halved after rollout.

Take conversational agents from prototype to production

Anees, on the App Store, prepares and records meetings, drafts the follow-up and acts only once the user approves, on the UAE sovereign cloud in Arabic and English. On Wakeel, agents build every option on the real app code next to how today's app behaves, the live app is never touched, every change sits behind a flag, and each task goes to engineering with what users see, the data, states and exceptions, and done criteria.

Lead a cross-functional team across a full product line

It launched on Meta Quest. In London I set up Kakao's first European subsidiary, with licensing and retail deals across the UK and France.

CookieRun VR

Make evaluation a first-class product practice

I designed a benchmark that gives Claude, GPT-4o and Gemini the same material and questions on memory.wiki: 95% correct for each, and 93.3% agreement between them. Before each test I set the primary metric and the guardrails, and every change is written down first with the metric to watch, a review date and the point at which it is switched off.

memory.wiki 03

Build product artifacts with AI tools yourself

I build my products in code with Claude Code, from the web to iPhone and Android apps, a CLI and an MCP server with 29 tools. On Wakeel, 94 options were built on the real app code over three reviews, and all 82 of my design comments came back as revised screens, with a median of 10 minutes.

Wakeel 03

Reimagine workflows with AI, and scale the practice across teams

About 50 AI agents with set roles run my studio's products: one sets priorities, one is the only one allowed to deploy, and others handle finance, data, funnel and pricing, inside daily ad and loss caps. I split out deploy rights after agents were deploying 50 to 140 times a day. At Humain I set the experience principles for Humain One and ran design reviews against them, so the teams building on it worked to one standard.

Designing with AI

Communicate with extreme clarity, and drive consensus

On Wakeel each review becomes tasks, options and a document per task, and an update log of 253 entries records what changed and why. Decisions are settled by trying the real app screens together with the senior decision-maker who uses them, so the discussion runs on evidence rather than taste.

Wakeel 05

Hire and build an AI-capable cross-functional team

I have led teams of 6 to 25 and built three of them from scratch: the six-person experience team for Humain One, Saudi Arabia's national AI platform, the 25-person team behind Devsisters' VR game, and Kakao's first European subsidiary with eight. At Saudi Aramco I led a 12-person UX team.

Teams

Drive a step change in product performance

On Paldo Saju, showing the real prices in place of a struck-through discount raised revenue per checkout visitor by 61%, in an A/B test of about 150 visitors per side. When the ads stopped paying back they came off, and the daily loss fell by 76%.

Paldo Saju 04

Preferred: conversational AI in regulated, mission-critical settings, used responsibly

Wakeel and Anees are government AI. In Wakeel a quick approve appears only when the checks raise no issue, and anything sent to Oracle waits five seconds with undo in the alert above. Anees records every action it takes. In London I led UX for Barclays mortgages and for Hello Bank!, BNP Paribas' mobile bank.

Wakeel 05

Where I would be stretching

My titles have been director and lead, not product manager, and owning the product has been the job: the VR game from first plan to launch, Humain One's product vision, and the prices, targets and stop rules of my own products. My experiments run at studio scale, about 150 people per side. At Meta's scale the samples grow and the method holds: set the metric and the stop point first, decide from graded output and funnels, and settle the statistics with data science.

03Products

What I build and run at Raymind.AI

Beyond productivity, I believe the most important use of AI is its most basic one: conversation that hears people's worries. Paldo Saju, TarotMasters and PastLife are different ways in, each starting from a narrow topic (saju, tarot, past lives) so it can hear worries in detail and answer precisely. What people worry about most, and which answers help them open up, became data for the next step, Auntie Rabbit.

memory.wiki

Gathers your AI conversations and documents at one address and hands them to any AI as context. Web, Mac, iPhone and Android apps, browser and VS Code extensions, CLI and MCP server, built on my own.

memory.wiki

Paldo Saju

An AI consultation that starts from saju: master characters from Korea's eight provinces hear your worry first, then talk it through with you. Fixing the checkout by the numbers raised revenue per checkout visitor 61%.

paldosaju.com/en

Auntie RabbitIn beta

A chat companion where Auntie Rabbit and her regulars listen to one person's story together. It is the next step, built on what the worries people shared on Paldo Saju and TarotMasters taught me, and it starts from the worry itself, without saju or tarot as the way in.

auntierabbit.com
