01What I bring

I design the assistant, and the system it speaks through.

  1. 01

    I design assistants that show what they are doing

    Wakeel's face is one orb in eight states: idle, listening, thinking, speaking, working, waiting for you, done and offline. The states read without labels at 58 points, one shader moves between them, and the orb stills under Reduce Motion. In Ask Wakeel it thinks and speaks in place of a sparkle icon and three dots.

    Wakeel, Presence
  2. 02

    I give what the model says a structure the screen can keep

    In Wakeel a recommendation opens to the depth its weight deserves: one block for a safe quick approve, the checks and what goes to Oracle for the rest, reasoning and conditions for a tender. For each decision, development gets the contract: whether quick approve is allowed, the checks, the depth and the approval order. Every action waits five seconds, with undo in the alert above.

    Wakeel 04
  3. 03

    I keep one system across the web, iOS and Android

    In memory.wiki one token source generates the light and dark colours as CSS for the web, desktop and VS Code, Swift for iOS and Kotlin for Android. When a palette kept by hand in four files drifted and a colour picked on Android silently failed to save, I made one JSON file the only place to change it. Seven of my apps are on Google Play.

    memory.wiki 03
  4. 04

    I prototype in code, and hand over specs engineers build from

    On Wakeel, agents build every option on the real app code, next to how today's app behaves. All 82 of my design comments came back as revised screens, with a median of 10 minutes. What is settled becomes one prototype and a document per task: what users see, what to build, the data, states and exceptions, and done criteria.

    Wakeel 03
  5. 05

    I have set the bar for systems other teams build on

    At Saudi Aramco I led a 12-person UX team and set up the company-wide design system and UX guidelines, used across business units. At Humain I built and led the six-person experience team for Humain One and ran its design system in Figma. Reviews are decided by shared standards, not by taste.

    Organisations and teams
  6. 06

    I am honest on the screens where money changes hands

    On Paldo Saju, removing a struck-through list price that had never been charged raised revenue per checkout visitor by 61%. In London I designed for Barclays mortgages and drew design principles for BNP Paribas' mobile bank, Hello Bank!

    Paldo Saju

Prototype in code: streaming, interruption, error recovery, latency

94 options built on the real app code over three reviews. Five seconds to undo any action, with four alert shapes switched on side by side in the same app, an offline state, and one shader moving between eight states. My own products I design and build in code.

Wakeel 03

A quality bar for component states, accessibility and micro-interactions

Counts hidden at 0, 99+ above 99, the last value kept while loading, and mirrored for Arabic. States that read without labels, and an orb that stills under Reduce Motion. A long-press preview with no buttons, so nothing gets approved by accident while reading.
