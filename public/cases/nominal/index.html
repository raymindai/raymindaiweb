What the role asks for, and where I have done it

Prototype agent products in days, in front of users before anything is specced

On Wakeel agents build every option on the real app code behind a flag, 94 over three reviews without touching the live app, and the senior decision-maker who uses it tries them on the real screens. All 82 of my design comments came back as revised screens with a median of 10 minutes, and the approval to Oracle went through 17 options in 8 days.

Wakeel, live prototype

Take the best ideas from zero to one, and ship them

I founded Raymind.AI in February 2026 and shipped ten products in six months, from the web to iPhone and Android. memory.wiki went from an idea to one knowledge base that Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor and Codex all read, through an MCP server with 29 tools, a CLI, VS Code and Chrome extensions and a Mac app.

memory.wiki 02

Watch people work, and fix where the agent gets in the way

A review of live IsekaiPlay chats found replies picked up the user's words 15 times out of 15 but drew the user on only 5; a rule that the first three replies end on the character's pull went from 6 of 15 to 15 of 15 in testing. On TarotMasters grading 85 readings showed 65% made up facts; fixing the cause cut it from 63% to 16% and 5% on the same 19 situations, and the live rate from 39.1% to 21.5%, short of the 10% target.

Chat products

Agent UX: stream the work, show every step, cite sources, let people take over

memory.wiki streams its answers as they are written, cites every claim back to the passage it came from, and says so when the knowledge base does not hold the answer. Each Wakeel card arrives with one key fact and a recommendation and opens to the depth its weight deserves; a quick approve appears only when the checks raise no issue, anything sent to Oracle waits five seconds with undo, and the finished state says what was done, where and when, and what happens next.

Wakeel 05

Agent-native workflows, not chat bolted onto old screens

In Wakeel ERP approvals moved onto the desk itself, each request at its own depth, and every action is logged. Anees, on the App Store, turns meetings into decisions, tasks and drafted follow-ups that run only once the user approves. A review tool I built sends each comment left on a page, with the sentence it is about, to the agent that made it: 255 notes to 28 agents in one week.

03Products

Everything I build and run at Raymind.AI

Beyond productivity, I believe the most important use of AI is its most basic one: conversation that hears people's worries. Paldo Saju, TarotMasters and PastLife are different ways in, each starting from a narrow topic (saju, tarot, past lives) so it can hear worries in detail and answer precisely. What people worry about most, and which answers help them open up, became data for the next step, Auntie Rabbit.

memory.wiki

Gathers your AI conversations and documents at one address and hands them to any AI as context. Web, Mac, iPhone and Android apps, browser and VS Code extensions, CLI and MCP server, built on my own.

memory.wiki

Auntie RabbitNew

A late-night snack stall where a rabbit auntie and a counter of AI regulars listen to one person together, by text or voice. Auntie remembers your story, and each night becomes a diary only you can see. Live on the web in Korean, English and Japanese; the iOS and Android apps are in store review.

auntierabbit.com

Paldo Saju

An AI consultation that starts from saju: master characters from Korea's eight provinces hear your worry first, then talk it through with you. Fixing the checkout by the numbers raised revenue per checkout visitor 61%.

paldosaju.com/en

TarotMasters

An AI consultation that starts from tarot: you pick one of fourteen masters, each with their own voice, tell them what is on your mind and read the cards together. In six languages.

tarotmasters.app

IsekaiPlay

Ten original stories told as audio drama, web novels and character chat on one account. Finishing an episode leads into a one-to-one chat with a character who remembers it.

isekaiplay.com

PastLife

An AI consultation that starts from past lives: Mir reads your story through tarot and shows the past life it finds, with a painted portrait and a 30-second film, then talks it through with you.

pastlife.app

dopaplay

A game platform where one account carries saves, rankings and purchases across four games, on the web and on phones: 3SEC FEED, DOPA DASH, POPCHAIN and Kai's Greedy Loop. Levels are tuned by bots, then checked against real play.

dopaplay.io

Screen Styler Pro

screenstyler.ai

jolong.aiBeta

A roast training platform, Duolingo for wit. It scores any content for clichés, pretension and AI slop, and trains you to give and take sharp feedback. Built on Roast Engine.

jolong.ai
