On Wakeel's desk, approvals from Oracle and requests from Outlook arrive together, and each card carries one key fact and Wakeel's recommendation.

A systems thinker on open-ended tools, not only A to B flows

memory.wiki gathers conversations and documents from ChatGPT, Claude, Notion and GitHub, splits them by heading, embeds them and adds a concept graph the model extracts, so any AI that reads them gets the same context. The page people read and the document an AI receives come from the same renderer and the same graph.

memory.wiki 03

A natural prototyper, in code and with the latest AI tools

I build my products in code with Claude Code, from the web to Mac, iPhone and Android apps, a CLI and an MCP server with 29 tools. On Wakeel, agents build every option on the real app code: 94 options over three reviews, and all 82 of my design comments came back as revised screens, with a median of 10 minutes.

Wakeel 03

Fit and finish: interaction, typography and motion

Wakeel's orb states read without a visible text label at 58 points, and one shader moves between them. Components have written states: counts hidden at 0, 99+ above 99, the last value kept while loading, and mirrored for Arabic.

Wakeel, components

A good citizen on Mac, iOS and the web

memory.wiki runs on the web, Mac, iPhone and Android, with Chrome, Safari and VS Code extensions. Colour, type, spacing, radii and motion live in one set of design tokens, generated as CSS for the web, the Mac app and VS Code, Swift for iOS and Kotlin for Android. On iPhone, Wakeel uses the system's own gestures and settings: a long press lifts a preview with no buttons, so nothing gets approved by accident while reading, and the orb stills under Reduce Motion.

memory.wiki 03

Evolve the brand and design language

For Wakeel's face I set five criteria, among them calm enough for senior UAE government officials and national symbols used sparingly. With the agents we drew six directions live in the browser, ran each through the same eight states and chose Midad, Arabic for ink: ink inside glass.

Wakeel, Presence

Hours with users, and a sense of what to build next

In Wakeel reviews we settle options by trying the real app screens together with the senior decision-maker who uses them. I read review notes for the needs underneath: the first review came down to four needs, and its nine tasks were solved in that order. On Paldo Saju I measured every step of the funnel: each kept 57 to 92% of the step before, except the checkout button at 13%, so the experiments went there.

Wakeel 03

Measured on what shipped, not on a Figma file

On Paldo Saju, showing the real prices in place of a struck-through discount raised revenue per checkout visitor by 61%, and a "less friction" first screen that lost to the numbers was reverted that night. On TarotMasters, grading 85 readings showed that 65% contained made-up facts; on a test sample, a rule that only what the user said counts as fact cut that rate from 70% to 30% before it went out to everyone.

Paldo Saju 04

Scrappy when exploring, precise once there is conviction

Wakeel's approval to Oracle went through 17 options in 8 days, each on the same app code, before we settled on the last one. Then the detail decided it: every action waits five seconds, with undo in the alert above, and finished work shows as a state, "Approved in Oracle", with nothing left to press.

Wakeel 05

Shape an early team's design DNA

I have built three teams from scratch: the six-person experience team for Humain One, where I set the experience principles and the review system; the 25-person team behind Devsisters' VR game; and Kakao's first European subsidiary. At my own studio, Raymind.AI, I shipped ten products in six months, and every change starts with the metric to watch, a review date and the point at which it is switched off.

03Products

What I build and run at Raymind.AI

Beyond productivity, I believe the most important use of AI is its most basic one: conversation that hears people's worries. Paldo Saju, TarotMasters and PastLife are different ways in, each starting from a narrow topic (saju, tarot, past lives) so it can hear worries in detail and answer precisely. What people worry about most, and which answers help them open up, became data for the next step, Auntie Rabbit.

memory.wiki

Gathers your AI conversations and documents at one address and hands them to any AI as context. Web, Mac, iPhone and Android apps, browser and VS Code extensions, CLI and MCP server, built on my own.

memory.wiki

Paldo Saju

An AI consultation that starts from saju: master characters from Korea's eight provinces hear your worry first, then talk it through with you. Fixing the checkout by the numbers raised revenue per checkout visitor 61%.

paldosaju.com

Auntie RabbitIn beta

A chat companion where Auntie Rabbit and her regulars listen to one person's story together. It is the next step, built on what the worries people shared on Paldo Saju and TarotMasters taught me, and it starts from the worry itself, without saju or tarot as the way in.
