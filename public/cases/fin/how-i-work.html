How I work

Build small, judge by numbers,
keep a record that compounds.

I believe a product leader's job is less about shipping one good feature and more about building a way of working that keeps producing good decisions. I refined it leading teams of 6 to 25 in large organisations, and I run my studio this way every week.

01How one improvement cycle runs

From choosing the problem to keeping the record, I run six steps every week.

  1. 1

    Read the numbers

    From a metric tree that splits weekly revenue into new visits, conversion rate, order value and orders per buyer, and from the step-by-step funnel, I pick the biggest leak.

  2. 2

    Write the hypothesis

    Before changing anything, I write what will change, the primary metric, the guardrails, the decision date and the stop criteria in the change log. One test per surface at a time.

  3. 3

    Build it myself

    I do not stop at mock-ups. I build working screens with Claude Code, so reviews happen on screens you can hold in your hand, not on static images.

  4. 4

    Review on real screens

    I set the release checks before launch and review phone captures down to overflow and line breaks. I sign off every payment screen from the captures, and feedback left on the review page goes straight to whoever made the screen.

  5. 5

    A/B test

    Traffic is split into two groups by device and compared over the same period, with the primary metric and the guardrails watched together. With small samples, I roll out only when every metric points the same way.

  6. 6

    Decide and record

    I roll the change out or revert it, and every Monday I group the week into what changed, what happened, what I learned and what comes next. As the record builds up, the next decision comes faster.

02UX principles

I set principles first, so the team judges by the same standards.

Show things as they are

Show only the prices and terms that actually apply. One honest line sold more than a dressed-up discount.

Paldo Saju +61%

Give before you ask

Before any step that asks something of users, put something they get first.

Paldo Saju conversion 5.8% → 9.2%

AI acts only after approval

When AI acts on a user's behalf, it does so only after the user confirms, and every action is recorded.

Anees, Abu Dhabi Government

The same wherever you look

Same data, same sentences, same renderer. Consistency is made in the system, not on the screen.

One test per surface at a time

Any surface where money is at stake gets a single experiment, and the decision criteria are set before it goes live.

03Designing with AI

From design to launch and operations, I work with AI as part of the team.

I build products myself with Claude Code, from screen design through to launch. Operations are run by AI agents, each given its own role: around 50 across the studio, around 30 of them on Paldo Saju. One agent sets priorities, one alone holds deployment rights, and agents for finance, data, the funnel and pricing each work in their own lane.

People decide three things: prices, new paid channels and budget increases. Agents cannot go past the daily ad cap, the loss cap or the limits on budget changes (20% up, 30% down). Review feedback is left right on the page, and it goes straight to the agent that made it.
