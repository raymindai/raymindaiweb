memory.wiki

Define new interaction patterns for an AI agent

In Wakeel the button is the recommendation, and how Wakeel thought (the context, what it weighed and the evidence) opens beneath it. A quick approve appears only when the checks raise no issue, each recommendation opens to the depth its weight deserves, and every action waits five seconds, with undo in the alert above. Its face is one orb in eight states, in place of a sparkle icon and three dots.

Wakeel 05

Design for desktop, mobile and chat

Ask Wakeel is a conversation inside a mobile app, where the assistant thinks and speaks. Paldo Saju and TarotMasters are chat consultations on the web. memory.wiki is a desktop and mobile tool that AIs read too, through one URL and an MCP server.

Wakeel, live prototype

Balance speed with the last 1%

Wakeel's approval to Oracle went through 17 options in 8 days before we settled on the last one, A8. The undo alert now runs in four shapes side by side in the same app, to be settled in the next review. Then the details: counts hidden at 0, 99+ above 99, the last value kept while loading, an orb with its own offline state, and everything mirrored for Arabic.

Wakeel, components

Think end to end, from the system to the smallest interaction

For each Wakeel decision I define the contract between what the assistant says and how the screen renders it: whether quick approve is allowed, the checks, the depth and the approval order. At the other end, the Android colour picker in memory.wiki paints a chip, a link and a selected card in the chosen colour, so a tap shows what is about to change.

Wakeel 05

Build prototypes to communicate ideas

On Wakeel, agents build every option on the real app code, next to how today's app behaves: 94 options over three reviews. All 82 of my design comments came back as revised screens, with a median of 10 minutes. The live prototype runs listening, answering and speaking end to end.

Wakeel 03

Taste, in interaction and visual design

Wakeel's orb states read without a visible text label at 58 points, one shader moves between them, and the orb stills under Reduce Motion. A long press lifts a preview with no buttons, so nothing gets approved by accident while reading.

Wakeel, Presence

Tell a compelling story about the decisions

Wakeel's approval to Oracle started from one line in the first review: approving a purchase request ended on "Everyone has been notified", and nothing said the work had moved on in Oracle. After 17 options the card's button reads "Approved in Oracle" and the page shows only what was done, where and when, and what happens next. An update log of 253 entries records what changed and why.

03Products

What I build and run at Raymind.AI

Beyond productivity, I believe the most important use of AI is its most basic one: conversation that hears people's worries. Paldo Saju, TarotMasters and PastLife are different ways in, each starting from a narrow topic (saju, tarot, past lives) so it can hear worries in detail and answer precisely. What people worry about most, and which answers help them open up, became data for the next step, Auntie Rabbit.

memory.wiki

Gathers your AI conversations and documents at one address and hands them to any AI as context. Web, Mac, iPhone and Android apps, browser and VS Code extensions, CLI and MCP server, built on my own.

memory.wiki

Paldo Saju

An AI consultation that starts from saju: master characters from Korea's eight provinces hear your worry first, then talk it through with you. Fixing the checkout by the numbers raised revenue per checkout visitor 61%.
