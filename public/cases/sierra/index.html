Wakeel 03

Conversation design for complex, high-stakes and regulated interactions

In Wakeel an approval cannot be taken back once it reaches Oracle. A quick approve appears only when the checks raise no issue, anything sent to Oracle waits five seconds with undo in the alert above, and the finished state reads "Approved in Oracle" with only what was done, where and when, and what happens next. That flow went through 17 options in 8 days. In London I led UX for Barclays mortgages and for Hello Bank!, BNP Paribas' mobile bank.

Wakeel 05

A trusted advisor to senior leaders

Wakeel serves a senior decision-maker in the Abu Dhabi Government, and its options are settled by trying the real app screens together with that person, so the discussion runs on evidence rather than taste. At Devsisters I pitched a CookieRun VR game inside the company and to Meta, won the investment and negotiated the company's first platform partnership with Meta.

Wakeel 03

Working sessions that keep the build on track for launch

Each Wakeel review becomes tasks, a comment thread that moves to done, one combined prototype and a document per task for engineering. All 82 of my design comments came back as revised screens with a median of 10 minutes, and an update log of 253 entries records what changed and why.

Wakeel 03

Define the evaluation and quality bar

I designed a benchmark that gives Claude, GPT-4o and Gemini the same material and questions on memory.wiki: 95% correct for each, and 93.3% agreement between them. On TarotMasters, grading 85 readings showed that 65% contained made-up facts. Rules alone barely moved it, so I fixed the cause (a later instruction was overriding the source rule): on the same 19 situations the rate fell from 63% to 16% and 5% (two graders), and across all live readings from 39.1% to 21.5%, still short of the 10% target.

memory.wiki 03

Then lift the outcome week over week

On Paldo Saju every change is written down first with the metric to watch, a review date and the point at which it is switched off: eight checkout tests in three weeks, six kept and two rolled back. The last free answer now ends with two blurred lines that open when you leave an email, and the payment rate rose from 5.8% to 9.2% in an A/B test. On IsekaiPlay a rule that the first three replies end on the character reaching out went from 6 of 15 replies to 15 of 15 in testing.

Paldo Saju 04

Directly impact the product from customer work

On dopaplay.io I pulled the login, saves, rankings and remote config that four games each kept on their own into one platform, so a new game connects with one line of SDK. memory.wiki gives Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor and Codex the same knowledge base through an MCP server with 29 tools.

memory.wiki 03

Technical aptitude: APIs, data models and architecture

memory.wiki embeds documents into pgvector and returns them with a concept graph the model extracts; documents that have not changed are not embedded again. The IsekaiPlay story engine gives each job, from classifying a message to writing a key scene, to a model tier in one config file, and can switch it between Google, OpenAI, Anthropic and DeepSeek. Prompt caching cut the cost of each LLM call by 19.5%.

03Products

What I build and run at Raymind.AI

Beyond productivity, I believe the most important use of AI is its most basic one: conversation that hears people's worries. Paldo Saju, TarotMasters and PastLife are different ways in, each starting from a narrow topic (saju, tarot, past lives) so it can hear worries in detail and answer precisely. What people worry about most, and which answers help them open up, became data for the next step, Auntie Rabbit.

memory.wiki

Gathers your AI conversations and documents at one address and hands them to any AI as context. Web, Mac, iPhone and Android apps, browser and VS Code extensions, CLI and MCP server, built on my own.

Paldo Saju

An AI consultation that starts from saju: master characters from Korea's eight provinces hear your worry first, then talk it through with you. Fixing the checkout by the numbers raised revenue per checkout visitor 61%.
