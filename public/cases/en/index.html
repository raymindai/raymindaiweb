01What I bring

The work products need in the age of AI, I have already done myself.

  1. 01

    A leader who has built AI products from start to finish

    I do not stop at mock-ups. I design AI products, build and launch them myself, and run them. Paldo Saju, memory.wiki and Auntie Rabbit are all solo builds, with a team of AI agents sharing the operations. I know first-hand what AI does well and where it gets things wrong.

    Case studies
  2. 02

    I judge the screens where money is at stake by the numbers

    Even for a single checkout screen, I set the decision metric and guardrails first, then improve it through A/B tests. Showing the price truthfully raised revenue per checkout visitor by 61%, and even a winning experiment has its rollback criteria set in advance.

    Paldo Saju
  3. 03

    I have worked across many countries and industries

    In London I built digital experiences for Nike, Barclays, BNP Paribas and Volvo. In Saudi Arabia and Abu Dhabi I designed AI products for governments and companies. I work in Korean and English, and I keep users from different cultures in mind together, within one product.

  4. 04

    I make content that wins people over

    I made dopaplay, a game platform where one account plays several games. I made IsekaiPlay, with audio dramas and character chat, and Auntie Rabbit, where a cast of AI characters listens to your worries together. I know how to put fun and trust into one experience.

  5. 05

03Products

Products running now

Auntie RabbitIn beta

A chat-based, one-to-many companion where Auntie Rabbit and her regulars listen to one person's story together. It grew out of what I learned from the worries people actually shared on Paldo Saju and TarotMasters, and it focuses on the worry itself more than on fortune-telling.

Paldo Saju

AI saju consultation: masters from Korea's eight provinces hear your worry first, then talk you through the saju drawn from your birthday. Fixing the checkout by the numbers raised revenue per checkout visitor 61%.

dopaplay

A game platform that gathers the games I make in one place. With one account you can play 3SEC FEED, a feed of three-second mini-games that speeds up as you swipe; DOPA DASH, a rhythm arcade; POPCHAIN, a jelly-pop puzzle; and GREEDY LOOP, a one-thumb arcade.

TarotMasters

AI tarot consultation. Placing KakaoPay, Naver Pay and card side by side on the checkout screen raised the payment completion rate from 54% to 80%.

IsekaiPlay

Original stories told as audio dramas, web novels and character chat, all joined by one account.

memory.wiki

Gathers your AI conversations and documents at one address and hands them to any AI as context. Web, Mac and iPhone apps, browser and VS Code extensions, CLI and MCP server, built on my own.

PastLife

From one selfie, five past lives as portraits, cinematic videos and a short film, then a chat with who you were. Solo, couples and pets, in nine languages.

Screen Styler Pro

A Mac app that unlocks the locked HDR peak brightness of your display up to 1600 nits, and grades contrast, colour and screen effects like editing a photo. Pick a preset for each connected display.
